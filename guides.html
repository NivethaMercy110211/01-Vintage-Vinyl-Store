Step By Step

First-Time Turntable Setup

Follow these six steps to go from unboxing to your first record.

1

Level the Plinth

Place your turntable on a stable, level surface away from speakers. Use a bubble level on the platter — slight adjustments at the feet prevent skating and uneven wear.

Stability 5 min
2

Install Belt & Platter

Loop the drive belt around the motor pulley and sub-platter, then seat the main platter. Spin by hand — it should rotate smoothly with light resistance.

Mechanical 10 min
3

Balance the Tonearm

Set counterweight so the arm floats horizontally, then dial tracking force per your cartridge spec (typically 1.5–2.5 g for entry-level carts).

Calibration 15 min
4

Connect Phono Output

Run RCA cables to a phono input or external preamp, then to your amplifier. Ground the turntable with the supplied wire to eliminate hum.

Wiring 10 min
5

Set Anti-Skate

Match anti-skate to your tracking force for even groove wear. Play a test record and listen for distortion in the inner grooves.

Fine-tuning 10 min
6

Drop the Needle

Clean your record, lower the cueing lever gently, and enjoy. Store the dust cover open during playback to avoid vibration.

Playback Ready
